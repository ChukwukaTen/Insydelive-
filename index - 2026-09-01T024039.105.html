1. Who can use InsydeLive

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. InsydeLive is a social app for nightlife venues and events — by using it you confirm you meet the minimum legal age for the country you're in, including for any venue you check into.

2. Your account

You're responsible for the activity on your account and for keeping your login credentials secure. You can request deletion of your account at any time from Profile → Privacy & data.

3. What InsydeLive is — and isn't

InsydeLive helps you see who's checked into a venue, discover events and gatherings, and connect with people — pass requests, gifts, follows, ticketed events, and private gatherings. InsydeLive does not sell, serve, or deliver alcohol or any other substance, is not a venue itself, and is not responsible for venue admission, safety, or conduct once you're there.

Some features involve real payments, described in Section 5 below. InsydeLive is a technology platform that facilitates these payments between users, organizers, venues, and gathering hosts using third-party payment processors — it is not a bank, is not itself a licensed payment institution, and does not hold your funds at any point. Money moves directly through our payment processors, Stripe and Paystack.

4. Conduct

Don't harass, threaten, or impersonate anyone; don't use the app to facilitate anything illegal; don't attempt to access another user's account or scrape the platform; don't submit fraudulent, misleading, or illegal content in an advertising request. We can suspend or remove accounts that violate this, and may withhold or reverse a pending payout where we reasonably suspect fraud, abuse, or a violation of these terms.

5. Payments and purchases

The following purchases involve real payment, processed by Stripe or Paystack depending on the currency involved. InsydeLive itself never sees or stores your full card number or bank login details — that's handled entirely by our payment processors.

• Event tickets — purchased from an organizer or venue hosting a ticketed event.
• Gifts sent to another user or a venue during a check-in (e.g. a drink, a round for the table).
• Gathering attendance fees — where a gathering host has chosen to charge guests to attend.
• Gathering cash gifts — optional, guest-chosen amounts sent to a gathering host.
• Gathering creation fees and event listing fees — a one-off fee some hosts and organizers pay to publish a paid event or gathering.
• Advertising requests — featured placement or a sponsored push notification for a venue, organizer, or event, described further in Section 7.

A live view of the total price is always shown before you pay, including our commission and the payment processor's own fee (Section 6). Payment is confirmed only once our payment processor confirms the charge succeeded — not merely when you tap "pay" — so if your connection drops before that confirmation, no ticket, gift, or fee payment is created and you should check your payment history before trying again.

6. Platform commission and payment processor fees

InsydeLive retains a 5% commission on ticket sales, venue gifts, and gathering attendance payments. Cash gifts sent to a gathering host carry no InsydeLive commission — the host receives the full amount, minus only the real fee charged by the payment processor. Gathering creation fees, event listing fees, and advertising payments are retained in full by InsydeLive, as they are fees for using the platform itself rather than payments passed on to another user.

In every case, the actual processing fee charged by Stripe or Paystack (which varies by card type, region, and processor) is also deducted before any amount is paid out to a host, organizer, or venue. This fee is set by our payment processors, not by InsydeLive, and can change if they change their pricing.

7. Advertising requests

Requesting featured placement or a sponsored push notification requires payment upfront, at the time you submit the request. Every request is then reviewed by InsydeLive before it goes live — payment does not itself make a promotion live. If your request is not approved, you are refunded in full automatically. Featured placement is priced per day for the duration you request; a sponsored push is a flat one-off price. Current pricing is shown in the app before you submit a request. Once a request has been approved and is live, it is not eligible for a refund.

8. Refunds

Our general refund approach, by payment type:

• Duplicate or accidental double charges are detected and refunded automatically in almost all cases.
• If an organizer or venue cancels a ticketed event, ticket holders are refunded in full.
• If a gathering host cancels a paid gathering, guests who paid to attend are refunded in full.
• If a guest cancels their own attendance at a paid gathering 24 hours or more before the event, they receive a 50% refund; inside 24 hours, no refund is given.
• Advertising requests that are rejected on review are refunded in full, as described in Section 7.
• Gifts and cash gifts are voluntary gestures sent directly to another person or venue and are non-refundable once sent, except in the case of a detected duplicate charge.

Refunds are issued back to your original payment method via Stripe or Paystack and can take several business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

9. Payouts to organizers, venues, and gathering hosts

If you receive money through InsydeLive — as an organizer, a venue owner, or a gathering host — you'll need to set up a payout account, which is handled by Stripe Connect or Paystack depending on your currency and country. By setting one up, you separately agree to Stripe's or Paystack's own applicable terms for connected accounts or payment recipients, in addition to these Terms. Full detail on how payouts work is set out in our separate Payments & Payouts Terms, which apply to you once you set up a payout account.

10. Safety

The optional emergency contact info you can add in Profile → Safety is for your own reference and is only shared with anyone else if you choose to press the SOS button. SOS notifies people currently at the same venue as you and opens a pre-written text to your emergency contact on your own phone — it is a best-effort tool, not a monitoring service, and does not replace calling emergency services directly if you're in immediate danger. We cannot guarantee anyone will see or respond to an SOS alert in time.

What we collect

Account info you provide directly: name, username, email, country, phone number (optional). Profile details you choose to add: bio, vibe, interests, photo. What InsydeLive does store: a record of what you purchased, the amount, the currency, the date, and a payment reference used to confirm the charge with our processor — this is your transaction history, visible to you in the app.

If you receive money through InsydeLive — as an organizer, venue owner, or gathering host — setting up a payout account means providing us with additional financial information, which we pass directly to Stripe or Paystack to set up your payout: your name, the bank account number and bank/sort code you want to be paid into (or a mobile money number and network, where supported), and the currency and country associated with that account. Location — only when you press SOS

We don't track your location in the background, ever, and check-ins are something you actively do, not passive tracking. The one exception: if you press the SOS button, we capture your device's location at that single moment to include in the alert sent to people at your venue. This is a one-time snapshot tied to that specific alert, not continuous tracking, and it only happens when you deliberately trigger it. Your browser will ask permission at that moment; if you decline, the SOS still sends, just without a location attached.

Outside of the SOS case above, we don't access your location, your contacts, photos beyond ones you choose to upload, or other apps on your device. We never collect or see your full card number or bank login credentials — see Payment and financial information above.

Stripe and Paystack are independent data controllers for the payment information they process directly — their own privacy policies govern how they handle it, and we encourage you to review them if you want the full picture of how they process payment data.

Draft — last updated August 2026. This has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet; treat it as a starting point before launch. It supplements, and should be read alongside, the main Terms of Service. 1. Who this applies to

This applies to you if you set up a payout account on InsydeLive — which happens if you're a venue owner receiving gifts, an organizer or venue selling tickets to an event, or a gathering host charging for attendance or receiving cash gifts.

2. InsydeLive is not your bank

InsydeLive is a technology platform, not a bank or a licensed payment institution. We never hold your money. Every payout is a direct transfer from our payment processor — Stripe or Paystack — to the bank account or mobile money account you've registered. InsydeLive's role is limited to calculating what you're owed, instructing our payment processor to send it, and giving you visibility into that process inside the app.

3. Setting up a payout account

Depending on your currency and country, your payout account is set up with one of two processors:

• Stripe (for GBP, EUR, USD, CAD, and AUD): you're taken to Stripe's own secure, hosted onboarding flow, where Stripe collects and verifies your identity and bank details directly. InsydeLive never sees your bank account number in this flow.
• Paystack (for NGN, GHS, ZAR, and KES): you enter your bank account number and bank (or, for Kenya, your mobile money number and network) directly in the app, which InsydeLive sends to Paystack to register you as a payment recipient. Where possible, we confirm the account name matches before saving your details.

By setting up a payout account with either processor, you separately agree to that processor's own applicable terms — Stripe's Connected Account Agreement, or Paystack's merchant/recipient terms — in addition to InsydeLive's Terms of Service and this document.

4. Accuracy of your details

You are solely responsible for the accuracy of the bank account, sort code/bank code, or mobile money details you provide. InsydeLive is not liable for funds sent to incorrect details you supplied, and we cannot always reverse a payout once your payment processor has sent it. If you notice incorrect details on file, update them in Payout details as soon as possible, before your next payout is processed.

5. How your earnings become a payout

Every payment that includes an amount owed to you — a ticket sale, a venue gift, a gathering attendance payment, or a gathering cash gift — creates a ledger entry the moment the payment is confirmed. That entry starts as pending, moves to ready once it clears our standard readiness checks, and is marked paid once the transfer to you has actually been sent. You can see this ledger, and your running totals by status, from Your earnings in the app.

Ready entries for the same payout account and currency are batched together and paid out on our standard automated schedule, as a single transfer rather than one transfer per transaction — this keeps transfer fees down and avoids you receiving many small separate payments for one busy night or event.

6. Holds and reviews

InsydeLive may place a hold on a ledger entry — delaying it from becoming payable — where we reasonably suspect fraud, a policy violation, a payment dispute, or another compliance concern, or where required by our payment processor. If your entry is placed on hold, you'll be able to see that status and, where given, the reason. A hold does not mean your money is forfeited; it means payment is paused pending review.

7. What you actually receive

The amount that reaches your payout account is the transaction amount, minus InsydeLive's platform commission (where applicable — see the main Terms, Section 6), minus the real processing fee charged by Stripe or Paystack for that specific transaction. This net amount is shown against each entry in your ledger, so what you see reflected is what's actually paid, not the gross transaction amount.

8. Onboarding status

A payout can't be sent until your payout account is fully set up and verified — a completed Stripe onboarding, or a verified Paystack bank/mobile money recipient. If a payout is ready but your account isn't yet verified, it's held until you complete setup; nothing is lost or expires while this is pending.
